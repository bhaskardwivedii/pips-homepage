# Story behind the build (draft, edit it into your own words)

**Track:** 1 · Pip, the skull baby (Junior Dream Engineer)

Everyone got the same baby, so I asked one question: what would Pip's homepage look like if Pip had built it himself in 2001, during naptime? The answer is a cozy GeoCities-style corner of the web. It has a marquee, a hit counter, a guestbook, a webring and 88×31 badges, but every piece of it is alive and playable. Pip is the host. He watches your cursor, narrates each section from a little bubble in the corner, falls asleep if you sit still too long, and giggles if you tickle him.

The portfolio is six working toys, not six pictures. You can play Pip's original lullaby on a Web Audio music box (or make him invent a new one). You can steer a nightlight through a dark room to switch on his lamp collection. You can scroll to fly his dream-delivery route, where windows light up as dreams arrive. You can flip through the storybook he "wrote and illustrated", and catch falling dreams in a mini-game. His origin story is told in five scroll-driven scenes instead of a wall of text.

**What I'm proudest of:**
- **Responsive Chaos Mode.** Pip names your screen size live (Pocket 🧸 → Whole House 🏠), reacts when you resize, and in 🌀 Chaos the windows tilt, wobble and become draggable while stickers appear wherever you click.
- **Every sound is synthesized.** There are no audio files at all, just oscillators, filtered noise and a generated reverb, with a mute button that is always visible.
- **Five Easter eggs** with a tracker, ending in a golden nightcap and a *Dream Detective* certificate.
- **Craft:** zero console errors at five screen sizes; Lighthouse 99–100 in every category on mobile and desktop; fully keyboard-accessible; respects reduced motion.

**Character:** canon Pip (cute skull face with glowing pinprick eyes, a tiny crooked smile, starry nightcap with a pink bow, Junior Dream Engineer). The art, lullabies and writing are all original and hand-made in SVG and CSS.
