<section 03 /> my story

How a baby got a job

  1. The quietest night

    Once, the Midnight Nursery was so quiet I could hear my own eyelashes. (I don't have eyelashes. That's how quiet.) Nobody could sleep.

  2. A broken music box

    In the attic I found a music box with a bent key. I hummed into it. It hummed back. Hehe.

  3. Three little ghosts

    Three grumpy ghosts floated by, wide awake. I played my wobbly tune. One yawned. Then two. Then… zzz.

  4. Hired!

    The Night Nanny gave me a starry nightcap, a lantern and a badge: Junior Dream Engineer. I wear it everywhere. Even in the bath.

  5. Tonight

    Now I fly over the rooftops, building dreams for everyone. Tonight's dream is a little different. It's for you. 💤
